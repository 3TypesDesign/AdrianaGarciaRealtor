Frequently asked questions

Buying, selling, ITIN, costs and areas

How much money do I need to buy my first home in Florida?

It depends on the loan type. Some FHA loans start at 3.5% down and some conventional loans start at 3% down for first-time buyers, plus closing costs. Florida also has down payment assistance programs.

In a free consultation we review your situation and I connect you with a lender so you know your real number.

Can I buy a home with an ITIN in Florida?

Yes. Some lenders offer ITIN loans. They usually require a larger down payment than a traditional loan and a solid income and payment history. I help you find the right lender and prepare your documents.

How much does it cost to sell my home?

Costs include the commission, which is negotiable, and the seller's closing costs. In the consultation I prepare a pricing analysis for your home and an estimate of what you would net after the sale.

Is there a cost for the consultation?

No. The first consultation is free with no obligation, in English or Spanish.

Which areas do you serve?

North Tampa Bay: Land O' Lakes, Wesley Chapel, Lutz, Odessa and the surrounding areas of Pasco and Hillsborough County.
